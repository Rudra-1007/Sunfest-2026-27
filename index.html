01 / THE IDEA

Energy is usually lost.
What if we harvested a little?

↯

The Problem

Vehicles repeatedly apply force to road surfaces. A small educational speed-breaker model can demonstrate how part of that mechanical motion may be converted into electrical energy rather than simply dissipated.

⚙

The Solution

A spring-loaded plate moves when an RC car crosses it. Piezo discs respond to controlled flexing while a rack drives gears and a DC motor used as a generator. Their outputs are conditioned before storage.

⌁

The Demonstration

Stored energy is measured and then used for low-power LEDs. The model is about energy conversion and smart infrastructure—not a claim that a small prototype can power full-size streetlights.

METHOD A
+−

Piezoelectric Harvesting

Piezoelectric discs generate electrical pulses when they flex. Rubber or foam contacts help transfer controlled movement without crushing the ceramic.

  • No rotating shaft required
  • Compact and easy to demonstrate
  • High voltage pulses, but low available energy
METHOD B
⚙⚙⚙

Rack & Pinion Generation

The moving plate drives a rack, rotating a pinion and gear train. The final gear spins a DC motor shaft so the motor acts as a small generator.

  • Converts linear travel to rotation
  • Gear ratio can increase shaft speed
  • Bearings reduce friction and improve alignment

04 / BUILD PROCESS

From empty board to working prototype.

01

Prepare the road base

Use a strong board, create the road surface, and leave a central opening for the movable speed-breaker assembly.

02

Install guide rods and springs

Fix guide rods vertically, place four springs under the plate, and restrict travel to only a few millimeters.

03

Mount piezo discs safely

Use controlled rubber/foam contact points. Never let the plate directly crush the ceramic discs.

04

Test the piezo section first

Rectify the output, connect a suitable capacitor, and verify a voltage change with a multimeter before adding more systems.

05

Add rack, pinion and gears

Attach the rack to the moving plate, mesh it with the pinion, support shafts with bearings, and align the gear train.

06

Connect and test the generator

Drive the DC motor shaft mechanically and test its output independently before combining energy paths.

07

Add storage and LED load

Observe capacitor polarity and voltage ratings. Keep the LEDs switched off while harvesting, then use stored energy for the demonstration.

08

Run the RC-car demonstration

Drive over the breaker repeatedly, observe mechanical movement and stored voltage, stop the car, then switch on the LEDs.

SYSTEM LOGIC

Electrical architecture

Build and verify each generation branch separately. Combining different sources requires appropriate rectification/isolation rather than directly tying arbitrary outputs together.

05 / ENGINEERING REALITY

Efficiency means reducing every avoidable loss.

↓

Reduce friction

Align gears and use bearings so less mechanical input is lost as friction.

↔

Optimize travel

Use enough plate movement to activate both mechanisms without making the obstacle excessive.

⚙

Tune the gear ratio

Balance rotational speed against torque. More speed is not automatically more useful output.

⚡

Minimize electrical loss

Use suitable rectification and efficient conversion electronics matched to the low-energy source.

IMPORTANT

Prototype ≠ power plant.

Piezo discs can show noticeable voltage while delivering little current. Large supercapacitors may therefore charge very slowly. Measure actual stored energy and treat the model as an energy-harvesting demonstration rather than claiming unrealistic streetlight-scale output.
