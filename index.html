STUDENT INNOVATION • 2026

Built at Sunbeam School Ayodhya

This security-surveillance prototype demonstrates how a tablet camera and computer vision can act as the robot's “eyes.” Instead of waiting for a conventional motion sensor to trigger, the system analyzes the camera view for a human and initiates a notification when a person is detected.

“Duty • Devotion • Discipline” — school values represented through purposeful innovation
THE IDEA

Security that can see.

HumanGuard AI focuses on visual human detection, fast alerts and accessible surveillance.

01
◉

Camera-first detection

The tablet camera provides a live visual feed. The detection software checks frames for a human rather than relying on a dedicated motion sensor.

02
⌁

Automatic alert

When the detection condition is met, the system can trigger a notification workflow so a connected device can inform the user.

03
◇

Practical surveillance

The concept can support homes, school spaces, offices, restricted areas and other locations where quick awareness matters.

SOCIAL IMPACT

Technology designed to help people.

🏠 Home awareness

Can alert residents when a person is visually detected near a monitored area, supporting quicker awareness.

🏫 School security

Demonstrates how computer vision can assist supervision of entrances, labs or restricted spaces.

🏢 Workplace monitoring

A scalable concept for observing selected areas where continuous human presence monitoring may be useful.

Responsible AI matters.

A real deployment should use clear consent and signage where appropriate, minimize stored footage, secure notifications and device access, and comply with applicable privacy rules. Human detection should support people—not replace responsible human judgment.

DESIGN ADVANTAGES

Why this approach is efficient.

VISUAL

Camera-centric

Uses the existing camera as the primary sensing input, reducing dependence on a separate PIR trigger.

SMART

Selective events

Detection logic can be configured around humans rather than treating every physical movement as equivalent.

MODULAR

Expandable

The concept can later add event logs, snapshots, multiple cameras, dashboards or autonomous patrol functions.
